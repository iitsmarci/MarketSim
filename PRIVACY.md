# Privacy & Security Policy

**MarketSim is a strictly offline-first, client-side application.**

We respect your privacy and have architected the software to ensure that your financial data remains entirely under your control.

## 1. Data Collection & Storage

- **Zero Telemetry:** MarketSim contains absolutely no tracking, no analytics, and no crash reporting software.
- **No Backend:** The application does not communicate with any external servers to perform its calculations.
- **Local Storage:** All scenarios, inputs, and settings are saved locally on your device (using browser IndexedDB or native local storage). We do not have access to your data.

## 2. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because MarketSim collects **zero** personal data and operates entirely within your browser or device, it inherently complies with the GDPR, CCPA, and other data privacy regulations. There are no cookies to accept, and no user accounts to manage.

## 3. Financial Disclaimer

MarketSim is an educational Monte Carlo simulator designed to help users understand probability and the mathematical ranges of possible outcomes.
It **does not** forecast markets, promise returns, or provide financial advice.
